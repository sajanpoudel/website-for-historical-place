Sites of the Sacred Garden

The Sacred Garden is the heart of Lumbini. These are the places most visitors walk to first.

Maya Devi Temple

The temple stands over the spot that is traditionally marked as the birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama. Excavated brick foundations and a marker stone can be seen inside.

Ashoka Pillar

Emperor Ashoka raised this sandstone pillar in the third century BCE. Its inscription records his visit and is one of the oldest written proofs of the site.

Puskarini Pond

The sacred pond next to the temple, where Queen Maya Devi is said to have bathed before the birth.

Monastic Zone

Monasteries built by Buddhist communities from many countries line two sides of the garden. They are open to visitors and show a wide range of building styles.
